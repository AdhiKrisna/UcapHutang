**Goal:** Bring every remaining screen in line with Apple's Human Interface Guidelines: semantic text styles instead of hard-coded sizes, semantic/asset colors that work in Dark Mode, 44 pt touch targets, native search, VoiceOver labels and grouping, Dynamic Type that wraps instead of shrinking, and Reduce Motion support — without changing app behavior or approved copy.

**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI (iOS 26.5), asset catalog colors, XCTest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-12-voice-draft-remediation-design.md` (§3, §11, §13, §14 P7, §15).

        XCTAssertEqual(Int((b * 255).rounded()), blue, file: file, line: line)
